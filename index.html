About us

Built on a founder's belief that green space serves people first.

Success is measured by what we give back to society and the environment. The guiding principle of Mr. Ramachandran Nair, Founder

Opal Landscaping was founded in Dubai in 2007 by the late Mr. Ramachandran Nair, an entrepreneur and philanthropist who believed the social and environmental value of a project mattered more than its size.

Today we are the flagship company of RC Group, led by Managing Director Mrs. Rajani Ramachandran. We carry the founder's principles into every contract: create landscapes that support wellbeing, help communities grow, and protect water and soil.

Our portfolio covers more than AED 550 million in completed projects and over AED 75 million in ongoing work, from single villa gardens to multi-zone maintenance contracts. We own our fleet, our equipment and our nursery supply, so we control quality and response time on every site.

FAQ

Common questions from Dubai clients.

What landscaping works best in Dubai's climate?

Drought-tolerant native and adapted plants, drip irrigation and heat-reflective paving. We match the planting to sun exposure and your water budget so the garden survives summer without heavy watering.

Can I use artificial grass?

Yes. It saves water and maintenance, but the product must be rated for high UV and heat. We supply heat-resistant turf and often combine it with natural planting for shade and cooling.

Do I need a permit for landscaping work?

It depends on the project size and your community or authority. We check the requirements during the site visit and handle the approval drawings for you.

How often does a garden need maintenance?

Most villa gardens need weekly or fortnightly visits; larger sites need daily teams. We set the frequency based on planting, irrigation and how you use the space.

How much does landscaping cost?

Cost depends on area, materials and features such as pools or pergolas. We give a written, itemised quotation after a free site visit.
